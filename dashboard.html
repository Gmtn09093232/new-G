Rules & Guidelines

Please read and follow the rules.

1. Account & Access
  • Register with valid phone and 4+ digit PIN.
  • Login with phone + PIN.
  • Only active admins can log in.
2. Dashboard Permissions
  • Players: view assigned players and balances.
  • Deposits: approve or reject with proof.
  • Withdrawals: approve or reject player requests.
  • Earnings: 40% commission on house profit.
3. Deposit Holding Limit
  • Total approved deposits must not exceed 1500 ETB.
  • System blocks approvals that exceed the limit.
  • Withdraw to Super Admin to reduce holding.
4. Admin Withdrawals
  • Request withdrawal of pending commission.
  • Provide amount, phone, method, and name.
  • Super Admin approves or rejects.
5. Game Participation
  • Admins can play as normal players.
  • Playing balance is separate from commission.
6. Profile & Payments
  • Each admin has a unique invite code.
  • Payment numbers shown to players for deposits.
7. Code of Conduct
  • Do not impersonate players or manipulate games.
  • Review deposit proofs honestly.
  • Report suspicious activity.
8. Key Limits
  • Deposit holding balance: 1500 ETB max
  • Commission rate: 40%
