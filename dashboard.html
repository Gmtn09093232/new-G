Rules & Guidelines / ህጎች እና መመሪያዎች

Please read follow the rules and guidelines below.

1. Admin Account & Access / የአድሚን መመዝገብያ
  • Register with valid phone and 4+ digit PIN.
  • Login using phone + PIN. Only active admins can log in.
  • Super Admin can activate or deactivate your account.
2. Dashboard – Permissions / ዳሽቦርድ – ፈቃዶች
  • Players: View your assigned players and their balances.
  • Deposits: Approve or reject deposit requests with photo proof.
  • Withdrawals: Approve or reject player withdrawal requests.
  • Earnings: Earn 40% commission on house profit from your players.
3. Deposit Holding Limit (Critical) / የተቀማጭ መጠን ገደብ (ወሳኝ)
  • Your total approved deposits must never exceed 1500 ETB.
  • If approval would exceed the limit, the system blocks it.
  • Request a withdrawal to the Super Admin to reduce your holding balance.
4. Admin Withdrawals / ገንዘብ መውጫ
  • Request withdrawal of your pending commission (available balance).
  • Provide amount, phone, method, and receiver name.
  • Super Admin approves or rejects your request.
5. Game Participation / በጨዋታ መሳተፍ
  • Admins can play as normal players using their Telegram account.
  • Playing balance is separate from commission earnings.
6. Profile & Payment Methods / ፕሮፋይል እና የክፍያ ዘዴዎች
  • Each admin has a unique invite code for players.
  • Payment numbers (Telebirr, CBE, M-Pesa) are shown to players for deposits.
7. Code of Conduct / የስነምግባር ደንቦች
  • Do not impersonate players or manipulate games.
  • Review deposit proofs honestly – fake approvals are a violation.
  • Report suspicious activity to the Super Admin.
8. Key Limits / ዋና ዋና ገደቦች
Deposit holding balance: 1500 ETB max
Commission rate: 40%
These rules and guidelines may be updated from time to time.
