Game Rules
75-ball Classic

🎲 How to Play

1. Choose your stake (10, 20, or 30 Birr).
2. Pick a BINGO card number from 1–100.
3. When the game starts, numbers are called every few seconds.
4. Mark the number on your card when it is called.
5. If you complete one of the winning patterns, tap 🏆 BINGO! immediately.

🏆 Winning Patterns

✅ 1 Row
✅ 1 Column
✅ 1 Diagonal
✅ 4 Corners

💡 Note: The centre FREE space counts as automatically marked.

🏆 Prize Rules

• If 2 or more players, 80% of the total entry fees goes to the winner(s).
• If only 1 player, you get 100% of your entry fee back if you call BINGO.
• If multiple winners, the prize is shared equally.

💰 Deposit

1. Tap ➕ Deposit.
2. Select a payment method (Telebirr, CBE Birr, or M-Pesa).
3. Send payment.
4. Tap Request Deposit.
5. Your balance updates after admin approval.

Minimum amount: 10 Birr

💸 Withdraw

1. Tap ➖ Withdraw.
2. Select a withdrawal method.
3. Enter your full name.
4. Enter your phone/account number.
5. Enter the amount to withdraw.
6. Tap Request Withdrawal.
7. Funds are sent after admin approval.

Minimum amount: 20 Birr
